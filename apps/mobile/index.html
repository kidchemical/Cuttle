Host is offline

Cuttle on your PC isn't reachable. Make sure the PC is on and Cuttle is running (tray icon), then retry, or tap Server to change the address.

Can't connect?
  1. Cuttle is running on the PC (tray icon).
  2. Phone / LAN access is on in Cuttle Settings.
  3. The phone is on the same Wi‑Fi, or Tailscale is up (use the 100.x or MagicDNS address).
  4. The firewall allows the port (8000 by default).

The phone is a remote window: Cuttle loads its UI from your PC inside this app, the same way the desktop app does.
